MindBoost

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

MindBoost is an educational brain-training game designed to help students practice memory, logical reasoning, and problem-solving through interactive games and activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use MindBoost.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MindBoost may process the following information:

  • Account information: Information such as your email address, display name, and authentication identifier when you create an account or sign in.
  • Game progress: Level completion, scores, correct and incorrect answers, accuracy, experience points, streaks, grades, and related game statistics.
  • Technical information: Information processed by authentication, hosting, or other services needed to operate and protect the game.

MindBoost does not intentionally request a user's precise location or collect payment card information as part of its core gameplay. Do not add this statement if your implemented features do otherwise.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Save game progress and display scores.
  • Track level completion and gameplay statistics.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the game.
  • Protect accounts and help prevent misuse.
  • Respond to privacy or account support requests.

3. Authentication Services

MindBoost may support email and password authentication, Google Sign-In, and Facebook Login. If you choose a third-party sign-in option, the relevant provider processes information according to its own privacy policy and settings.

Firebase Authentication may be used to authenticate users. Cloud Firestore may be used to store game progress and account-related records. Only describe services that are actually enabled and used by your published version of MindBoost.

Learn more about the providers' privacy practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

4. Storage and Security

Information is stored using the services configured for MindBoost, which may include Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Children and Student Privacy

MindBoost is intended for educational use, including by students. Users should provide only the information required to use the game. Parents, guardians, and educators should help younger students understand safe account use and online privacy.

If applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for the collection of a child's personal information, that consent must be obtained before the relevant collection takes place.

6. Sharing of Information

MindBoost does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that operate necessary features, such as authentication and cloud storage, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

Any sharing should be limited to what is necessary for the stated purpose and handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the game, maintain records, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information. Requests may be submitted using the contact details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MindBoost's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
