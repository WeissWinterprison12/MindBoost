Data Deletion Instructions

MindBoost respects users' privacy and provides a way to request deletion of an account and associated personal information.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to the MindBoost developer:

Email: shanezblankc@gmail.com

Use the subject line: MindBoost Data Deletion Request

Include the following information in your request:

  • The email address associated with your MindBoost account.
  • A statement that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
Important: Do not send your password or other sensitive login credentials by email.

What Happens Next?

  1. The developer will review your request and may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership.
  2. After verification, the developer will process the request and delete the account and associated personal data covered by the request, where applicable.
  3. Some records may need to be retained if required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable requirements.

What Data May Be Deleted?

Depending on the account and the information stored, deletion may include:

  • MindBoost account and authentication records.
  • Saved game progress and level completion records.
  • Scores, experience points, accuracy, streaks, and grades.
  • Other personal information associated with the account.

Deleting your MindBoost account does not automatically delete information held independently by Google or Meta. If you used one of those services to sign in, you may need to manage that provider's information separately.

Need Help?

For questions about a deletion request, contact shanezblankc@gmail.com.
